The Heavens were simple, about what you would expect. The city was all light blue bricks with gold accents. It was medieval, but also modern in a way. Vines crawled up pillars made of deep stained wood, and butterflies that had been crushed by quick feet or a sudden torrent. The air was a constant, crisp 75 degrees, save for the occasional dip during the night and winter. Rain was usually light, and infrequent. The Heavens were as close to real life as possible, except for the minor inconveniences such as searing heat or drowning rain. Everything that has ever died resides in the heavens. The Heavens are ruled by Rayalta, a dragon of radiant golden scales that reflect the air like a mirror. Rayalta is a mighty dragon, but such might is tiresome. Many see her as lazy, and many more think she is dying, but neither statements are true. One of the inhabitants of The Heavens thought her weakness was an opportunity, and his name was Ryze. Ryze was not human, nobody was, not even on the earth. Ryze was a backpack with a soft hue of the most pristine buttercups and the wings of a dove that had been kissed by the summer sky. But his appearance was deceiving, for his personality was the opposite. Ryze hated Rayalta, and his reasoning was irrational. He was jealous, she had all the power! Every time he went to greet Rayalta and ask for money, he was answered in condescending tones and passive aggressive answers. Rayalta knew that Ryze was a terrible person, and that his future wasn't very bright despite a full lifetime of character development. The guards had warned her about Ryze, and how he should be banished, but every time they warned her Rayalta responded with a finality that unnerved them even more. Rayalta knew what Ryze wanted, and he wanted power. But more specifically, he wanted her dead. Ryze was a rose with millions of tiny thorns. What you see on the surface is a confident man, someone with a kind heart and a soul that is pure, but Ryze was a corrupted, greedy, manipulative person. Ryze was pure evil. The only reason Ryze even got into The Heavens was because the earth God, Ireta, informed Rayalta of Ryze and the prophecy tied to him. Ireta is the parent of Rayalta, and her sister, Erital. Ireta created the earth, but she needed help taking care of the souls, so she gave the ownership and territory to them. If Ryze wanted to kill Rayalta, he would need something to kill her with, but where would he even start? There had to be some sort of weakness he could go off of. So, Ryze went off to see Rayalta. The morning air was quiet, yet full of life. Ryze bounded to the altar that Rayalta resided on. When he saw her, he asked one simple question. "O Rayalta, good morning! I have just one question for her royalty. May I see your scales? They are so captivating, I cannot help but stare!" Rayalta hummed a low tone, thinking for a moment. She responded with a booming base. "Yes, you may see my scales my child. They are free to look at, but do not be tranced for too long, my son." Ryze clasped his hands together and gave a toothy grin, before bowing in front of her. "Thank you, thank you my lord!" Ryze made his way to Rayalta's body, pretending to be admiring them. All he wanted was to see if there was any soft skin beneath her armor. He lifted a few scales, gasping dramatically to convince Rayalta that he really was just admiring her. Underneath her scales was in fact, velvet skin. He dared not touch it, but it looked fragile. "My lord, your scales are as pristine and gorgeous as ever. What is your secret?" Rayalta hummed an amused tone, before speaking once again. "My secret? There is no secret, my child. A mighty God like me keeps her scales perfect because she is a God! But that doesn't mean you cannot look as pretty as my scales. Now, come back tomorrow, and I will tell you the routine to keeping your fabric sharp and your feathers straight, there are others waiting in line for my guidance." Ryze bowed again, clasping his hands and displaying his sharp teeth. "Yes, my lord, I am very sorry. I will be back tomorrow! Take care, my lord!" And with that, Ryze bounded off once again. He had no plan on showing up tomorrow, he already had the perfect preening routine. With this new information, Ryze used all his brainpower to make a flawless plan. He knew that no normal sword nor precise arrow could kill her, so he went out for inspiration. In The Heavens, angels are forbidden from descending to the earth. But, this also includes the guards, so there's really only a punishment if you go back to The Heavens. Rayalta forbade all angels from going to earth, for if the mortals knew they would be judged, they would hide their true soul, and being dishonest is a sin. Therefore, Rayalta commanded that no mortal can know they exist for the sake of seeing everyone's true colors. Ryze wanted to go down to the earth because he knew that only mortals could create such morbid yet powerful contraptions to inflict pain upon each other. To be selfish is to survive in such a corrupted world. Now, Ryze was not stupid, but it could be debated because his intelligence was directly based off a 16 year old's intelligence, which may not be smart considering she went to Alpaca on May 20th and asked the lady for asparagus instead of green beans, and after being corrected by her father, she doubled down and said asparagus again. Anyway, embarrassing encounter aside, Ryze wasn't dumb. He knew that if he went down, he would be punished. His solution? Pretend he was doing it for the greater good. His idea was to make a scene (if anyone was around) by acting as if he saw someone fall or go down. But then what? Come back empty handed? He had to bring someone up in order for it to work… Adam was a victim of Ryze. Adam was a pencil, nothing more, nothing less. Adam and Ryze used to be best buddies, but that all ended when Ryze went too far. Ryze used to do pranks and mischievous things, but in an effort to make it more fun, he started doing terrible things. People would get hurt, and sometimes even hospitalized. Ryze loved it. Through this, he developed his snobby personality and manipulative words. He was the definition of a siren. Adam saw this, and he warned Ryze, but Ryze never listens to suggestions when he doesn't like them. Adam stopped being around Ryze, and he even avoided him. Adam finally got peace when Ryze died from his own greed. Ryze knocked on Adam's door with a false shyness. When Adam opened up the door, he was surprised, but also dreading the conversation. Ryze's body language radiated with a theatrical sorrow, and he spoke. "Hey Adam... I know you don't want to see me. But... I want to be friends with you again. I know I wasn't the best person back then, and I won't make those same mistakes again, I promise. I've learned from my past and I've used it as a learning experience to work on myself." Ryze's voice wavered and he fidgeted with his hands, refusing to look Adam in the eye. Adam's intuition was telling him to deny, but Ryze looked so convincing that he genuinely reconsidered. He thought for a moment, then he sighed and spoke. "Fine. But don't get any ideas, ok? I'm not helping you with anything, not until you've proven to me that you actually changed for the better." Ryze had predicted Adam wouldn't want to help him with anything, so he didn't panic. He had a feeling that gaining Adam's trust back would be quite the long performance, but he was ready to keep up the act for as long as it needed to take. Ryze smiled, making sure to humble his eyes. "Really??" Ryze lied. "Thank you!! I-" Ryze paused, before going to hug Adam. He stopped halfway there, pretending to realize that this may not be the time. Adam sighed again, but with a small smirk, before hugging Ryze. Adam let Ryze inside his house, turning on the TV to whatever Heaven Netflix adaptation of their favorite show was available. For the first time in a while, Ryze felt different. He was happy, genuinely happy. He liked the time he was spending with Adam, and he completely forgot about his plan. Ryze hated the feeling, and he hated how this was all built on another one of his lies. He could have easily dropped his plan, but that would require him to, well, drop his plan. To him, power was more important than anything. For 2 weeks, he kept up this act, and the perfect opportunity came when he was invited to hang out at a bar. His goal? Get Adam wasted, then drop him off the side. At the bar, Ryze saw someone he hadn’t seen in many years, his mother. He was genuinely terrified, because his mom was violent when she was drunk, and she abused him. She was up here because she had good intentions, but she only changed after she found out Ryze had died. Ryze hid behind Adam, who was also nervous. Unfortunately, she saw them both. She ran over to them and gave them both a big hug and kiss. Ryze flinched, refusing to greet her properly. His mom wasn’t surprised that Ryze was distant, but she was confused as to why it took so long to finally meet him. Ryze had actually moved very far away just to avoid his mom and Adam, and to also be in close proximity to Rayalta. Ryze’s mother, noticing that her son was genuinely uncomfortable around her, offered to leave. Adam accepted the offer as he didn’t want to ruin the day for Ryze, and she had asked. Ryze stared at his mother as she walked out. The plan was working great. Ryze kept offering Adam more drinks, and he drank them. Ryze was giddy with excitement, everything was perfect. Eventually, Adam wanted to go home, but he was too drunk to even move properly. Ryze offered to help him around. As they left, Ryze made his way to somewhere secluded and empty before just… gently nudging him. Adam fell, and he felt a sudden surge of… guilt? Something wasn’t right. Ryze flew down after Adam, attempting to catch him, but the wind was too strong to slow down after catching him. Ryze flailed his wings uselessly as they plummeted to the ground. Ryze winced, regretting this decision as he held onto Adam for dear life (or death? idk), shielding him with his wings. Adam was also panicking, but it was a drunk, dazed confusion more than anything. Ryze, for the first time without being told to, prayed. Ryze felt silly. Here he was, in the pursuit of power, falling to the ground holding his best friend, praying. Luckily, they both landed in a tree, so their fall wasn’t as lethal. Ryze hissed in pain as he felt the twigs and branches nip at his perfect feathers and kempt fabric. The fall was anything but graceful. Ryze tumbled on the leaf litter, covered in scars and bleeding. He looked like a wreck, how embarrassing! He felt his face get hot, he couldn’t let anyone see him like this! He brushed the dirt off himself, smearing the blood. He growled in rage and embarrassment, licking his fingers and wiping the blood clean. He spent so long cleaning himself and making sure he looked presentable, that Adam got up and just… stared at Ryze. He was still drunk, so he wasn’t exactly concerned about Ryze. Maybe Ryze should have shown up the next day to get tips on making sure he was clean from Rayalta. Ryze cursed under his breath, oblivious to Adam making his leave. When he finally gave up, he saw Adam stumbling away. Ryze called out after Adam, who didn’t look back. Ryze didn’t recognise the setting. He was in a city, yes, but where? It was late, the sky a pale blue and the setting sun painting the clouds in vibrant yellows and pinks. As Ryze guided Adam through the city, he couldn’t help but notice how disgusting everything was. The local homeless people stared him down, as if they wanted to skin him just for a blanket to wear at night. Ryze was extremely uncomfortable, and he picked up his pace. As Ryze was walking, someone tapped his shoulder. He turned around and scowled, biting back a lecture about ‘proper etiquette’ or common decency, but his face fell as he saw who it was. The person was definitely some sort of drug dealer. He didn’t want drugs, but people like this know about weapons and all the horrifying inventions to kill people. Ryze spoke, his voice jumping unexpectedly from the pent up anxiety. “I do not want drugs, please! I do not have anything for you, you… you… gah!” Ryze was rambling slightly, making the dealer a little confused. “Ok, I do not want drugs- but I do want to know… what’s the best way to… err… how do I say this… what’s the best way to penetrate armor, or at least sever a head?” The dealer’s eyes widened as Ryze changed the topic. “Uhh… I was never selling drugs, but…” Ryze interrupted the dealer. “I don’t believe you, not like I’d do anything to get you caught. I am no undercover police officer, I am just a humble little man helping my friend who got in a fight!” Ryze used a sort of saccharine tone, trying to convince the person. “Ok, fine. I don’t have the info you need, but I know a guy who knows a guy who knows a gal who knows a guy who knows a guy.” Ryze paused, his eyes wide. He shook his head before blurting out a “what???” Ryze found himself carrying a passed out Adam as he followed random people just to find the info he needed. Finally, after what felt like forever, he was standing in front of some guy who supposedly knew the info he so desperately craved. The room was dimly lit, casting a dramatic and cinematic filter over everything. He loved it. 2 shadowy silhouettes appeared in the light, confusing Ryze. 2 people? He thought it was just one? When he finally saw the whole person, it made a lot more sense. They were alligator clips, attached by a wire. They had horns, and matching orange hoods. Their names were Ali and Croc, how creative. Ali spoke, her voice raspy. “You need a little info, huh? We got it! What do you desire…” Ali leaned in, getting in Ryze’s personal space a little. He didn’t mind. “Do you know how to kill a dragon? I want her head.” Ali’s face fell as she looked at Croc without moving her head. Ali slowly recoiled back next to Croc. “Uhm… no? That’s… I’ve never seen a dragon, so I don’t know. But, assuming this is for some DnD campaign, I’d say uhh… a guillotine!” Croc sighed and pushed Ali in a brotherly manner. “How would you get a guillotine for a dragon? I’d say a precise cut from a sword would do the job.” Ali scoffed. “Well, you lure it into the guillotine, and then chop!” Croc huffed, genuinely a little peeved. “That’s- no! If we’re being genuine here, I’d say get behind the thing, or beside it, and use a spear to get under its scales. Once that kills it, then you can take its head. None of this… guillotine bullshit.” As much as Ryze hated to admit it, the idea wasn’t bad. He was just hoping for something more… epic, valiant, something that looked amazing. But this will have to do. Ryze thanked Ali and Croc for their help before flying back up to The Heavens. Before landing, Ryze had to check for anyone that might see him. He saw no one, but just in case, he dramatically heaved Adam up while exhaling and falling onto the ground with a theatrical grace. Ryze was genuinely tired, for he was out of shape and had never had to carry anyone before. Ryze grabbed Adam and practically dragged him to his house. Since he was already pissed off from having to carry Adam, he dropped Adam on his bed and left him. Ryze walked home. Ryze was exhausted, so he didn’t even bother to preen himself before going to bed. When Ryze woke up, the first thing he felt was the ache in his wings and arms. How was he supposed to defeat Rayalta when he can’t even get out of bed without wincing? How could he even be a king if he’s so weak? But then, he remembered how in years prior, kings and queens weren’t even teenagers before they were handed a crown. Ryze felt rejuvenated at this thought, he was fit! And he even had the corrupt mind of a king! Huh? Who said that? Wasn’t me 👀💧anyway. Ryze was still in pain, so he decided to not go too far with the day’s duties. He wrote a list down on the magnetic notepad that hung on his fridge. #1: Spear. #2: Learn how to spear. #3: King!!!!!!!!! Yeah that’s it. Ryze made his way to the nearest home improvement store to find a shovel and modify it to become a spear. At the store, he bought a few different things, like a shovel, a trowel, sticks, rope, and some screws. He also bought a knife sharpening tool. At home, he decided to use the trowel as the spear and the sticks to make it reach. Ryze failed miserably, and he ended up just putting a steak knife on one of the sticks and called it a day. Ryze was lazy, and he knew that a shovel wouldn’t peirce skin as easily as a steak knife. Plus, steak knives are meant to slice through meat, it’s in the name after all. Ryze ended up returning everything he didn’t use. He was still very achey, so he rested for the next few days while binge watching videos on how to fence or anything that might be remotely useful. Surprisingly, it was actually helpful and he learned a lot. Once he felt better, he practiced for one full day, just to get used to the feeling of the makeshift spear. The next day, he went out. He was extremely nervous, what if the guards got him and jailed him? What if it failed? What if- what if- what if. Ryze steeled his nerves and hyped himself up. There was no turning back now. Ryze made his way to Rayalta’s shrine. He had put a cloth over his spear so it looked less like a spear and more like a random unidentifiable tool or a sack of some sort. Unbeknownst to Ryze, Rayalta knew today was the day, and she had told her guards to stay out of the situation. As confident as Ryze looked, he was extremely nervous, his anxiety at an all time high. Ryze bypassed the long line in front of Rayalta, causing many to bark at him with rage and protest. He didn’t care. When he made it to Rayalta, he pushed the current speaker out of the way, interrupting them. He dared not speak, because if he did, the guards would surely grab him after learning his motive. Ryze pulled the cloth off his spear, the blade glinting in the radiance of Rayalta. For the first time in many many years, Rayalta moved. They brought up her head to gaze upon Ryze as he sprinted towards Rayalta. As much as Rayalta didn’t want to give Ryze what he wanted, they knew that everyone would see her as weak and unfit, therefore congratulating Ryze for his achievements. This was proving to be more difficult than he thought, but he mustn't give up! Rayalta growled and spoke to Ryze with her booming voice. “You dare attack me? Have you no respect for your lord? My son, I knew you were better than this. How could you betray me?” Ryze got up and roared back at Rayalta. “You are nothing but a weak dragon! You have no power, you are no god! You cannot even move to save yourself from my wraith!” As predicted by Rayalta, few from the crowd agreed with Ryze. Rayalta hissed and stretched out her body.
